Safety

A house designed
for peace of mind.

Safety is non-negotiable. The protocols below have produced zero incidents in the last three years — a record we'd rather brag about quietly than loudly.

  • 24/7 trained security staff
    Two on-shift guards at all hours, with regular professional training.
  • Full-coverage CCTV
    All entries, hallways, and common areas under continuous monitored recording.
  • Strict access control
    Residents-only entry beyond the office. No male visitors past the front desk — no exceptions.
  • Parent line, always open
    Direct office contact for parents — for check-ins, leave coordination, or anything at all.
